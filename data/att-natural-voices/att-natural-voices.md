## Overview

AT&T Natural Voices is a proprietary text-to-speech system developed by AT&T Labs.

## Technical Details

- **First public release date**: Unknown
- **Latest stable version**: 2008
- **License**: Proprietary
- **Available languages**: English (British), English (Indian), English (US), French, French (Canadian), German, Italian, Spanish (Latin American)
- **Available voices**: 20
- **Programming language**: C++
- **Operating system(s)**: Linux, Windows
