## Overview

TTS Arena is a community-driven leaderboard operated by Artificial Analysis that evaluates text-to-speech models through blind A/B testing. Users compare model outputs side by side to rank naturalness and quality across different TTS systems.

## Features

- Blind A/B testing of TTS model outputs
- Community-driven rankings and leaderboards
- Comparative quality scores across multiple models
- Regular updates with new model submissions

## Pricing

Free to use for browsing and participating in benchmarks.
