## Overview

TTSMaker is a free AI voice generation platform designed for users working on a budget or testing AI voice technology without requiring a credit card.

## Key Features

- More than 600 voices across 100+ languages
- 20 voices available for unlimited free generation
- 20,000-character weekly limit for premium voices
- Voice speed adjustment, pitch tweaks, and paragraph pause timing
- Background music upload and layering
- Multiple export formats: MP3, OGG, AAC, OPUS, WAV
- SRT subtitle file generation alongside audio
- Commercial use allowed even for free voices
- Files stored for 30 minutes before deletion

## Pricing

- Free: unlimited voices (20 available) and 20,000 characters/week for premium voices
- Lite plan: $13.99/month, unlocks 300,000 characters monthly

## Deployment

Cloud-based (Web).
