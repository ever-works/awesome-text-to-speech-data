## Overview

VoiceText is a proprietary text-to-speech system developed by ReadSpeaker (formerly NeoSpeech).

## Technical Details

- **First public release date**: 2002
- **Latest stable version**: 2017
- **License**: Proprietary
- **Available languages**: English (US), English (British), American Spanish, Canadian French, Chinese Mandarin, Japanese, Korean
- **Available voices**: 13
- **Programming language**: C/C++/Java
- **Operating system(s)**: Windows, Linux

## Technical Voice Details

- SSML support: Yes
- SAPI version: 5.x
