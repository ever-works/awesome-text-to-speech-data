## Overview

Cepstral is a proprietary text-to-speech system developed by Cepstral LLC.

## Technical Details

- **First public release date**: 2000
- **Latest stable version**: 2013
- **License**: Proprietary
- **Available languages**: English (British), English (US), Italian, French (Canadian), German, Spanish (American), ...
- **Available voices**: 25+
- **Programming language**: C/C++
- **Operating system(s)**: Mac OS X, Windows, i386-Linux, x86-64-Linux, Sparc-Solaris, i386-Solaris

## Technical Voice Details

- SSML support: Yes
- SAPI version: 5.x
- WS support: Yes
- PLS support: Yes
- CLI support: Yes
