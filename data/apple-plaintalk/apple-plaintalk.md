## Overview

Apple PlainTalk is Apple's speech synthesis technology, first introduced in 1984 and bundled with Mac OS X.

## Technical Details

- **Latest stable version**: 2018
- **License**: Bundled with Mac OS X (Proprietary)
- **Available languages**: English (United States), ...
- **Available voices**: 15+
- **Operating system(s)**: Macintosh
- **Programming language**: Not specified

## Features

- System-level speech synthesis for macOS
- Bundled with the operating system at no additional cost
