## Overview

Coqui TTS is an open-source text-to-speech library supporting 1100+ languages, zero-shot voice cloning, and fine-tuning. Originally developed by Coqui AI, it is now community-maintained after the company shut down in late 2023.

## License

Community-maintained (originally under Coqui AI)

## Deployment

Self-hosted, local
