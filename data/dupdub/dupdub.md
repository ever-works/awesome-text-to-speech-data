## Overview

DupDub is an AI voice generation platform that emphasizes phoneme-level pronunciation control, making it suitable for technical jargon, foreign brand names, and industry acronyms.

## Key Features

- Over 750 voices available in 1,000 styles and 90 languages
- Phoneme-level pronunciation controls via on-screen phonetic keyboard
- Control over pitch, rhythm, speed (general and section-by-section)
- Acronym pronunciation options (as word or individual letters)
- Granular timing controls for punctuation and paragraphs
- All-in-one content workflow: script generation, voiceover, built-in video editor
- Web, iOS, and Android apps

## Pricing

- Free 3-day trial with 10 credits
- Personal plan: $15/month with 150 credits per month
- Pay-as-you-go plan available

## Deployment

Cloud-based (Web, iOS, Android).
